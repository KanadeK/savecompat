# Code of conduct

SaveCompat contributors are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and productive.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and precise when discussing code and game-data risks.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Welcome contributors of different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Respect privacy, especially when examples involve player saves.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and release readiness.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention;
- publishing another person's private information or save data;
- deliberately introducing destructive behavior, malware, or cheats;
- sustained disruption, insults, or bad-faith review; and
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact methods on the GitHub profile.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone represents the project.
